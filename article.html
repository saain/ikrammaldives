The first thing every believer must know

'Aqīdah · Foundations6 min readPublished recently

Before the prayer, before the fast, before any act we offer — there is one question that shapes everything: Do we know the One we are worshipping?

Knowledge before action

The scholars placed a chapter in their books titled "Knowledge comes before speech and action," drawing from the verse where Allah commands the Prophet ﷺ to first know, and then to seek forgiveness. Knowing Allah is the root from which every branch of worship grows.

"So know that there is no deity except Allah, and ask forgiveness for your sin." — Sūrah Muḥammad · 47:19

What it means to "know" Allah

To know Allah is to know Him through His names, His attributes, and His actions — as He described Himself and as His Messenger ﷺ described Him. This knowledge is not abstract; it changes how we hope, how we fear, and how we live.

A heart that recognises its Lord

When the heart truly recognises the Most Merciful, worship stops being a burden and becomes a longing. The believer who knows that Allah is السميع البصير — the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing — never feels alone, and never feels unseen.
