رَّبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا

“My Lord, increase me in knowledge.”

Sūrah Ṭā Hā · 20:114

Ilm · العِلْم

Studies, histories, lessons and reference works — written from the primary sources, reviewed before publication, and offered freely to the community.

The library grows section by section. Each work states its sources, notes where scholars differ, and passes through review before it is published under IKRAM’s name.

Now open

Seerah السيرة

The life of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ studied from the classical sources. The first series, Al-Maghāzī, follows the campaigns from Badr to Tabuk — each entry with its dates, its Qur’ānic passages, and the lessons the scholars have drawn.
