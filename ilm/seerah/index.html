Al-Maghāzī — the campaigns of the Prophet ﷺ

From Badr to Tabuk, each campaign studied from the classical sources — its dates in both calendars, the Qur’ānic passages revealed in connection with it, and the lessons the scholars have drawn. Where the sources differ, we say so.

Entry 1 · Ramadan 2 AH

Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā

Friday, 17 Ramadan 2 AH · March 624 CE

A little over three hundred believers met an army of Quraysh nearly a thousand strong at the wells of Badr — and the Qur’ān named the day Yawm al-Furqān, the Day of the Criterion. The consultation, the wells, the night of prayer, the victory, and the mercy shown to the captives.

The wells of Badr Sūrat al-Anfāl 14 martyrs · Badriyyūn
