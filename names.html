Al-Asmā' al-Ḥusnā

The 99 names of Allah

"And to Allah belong the most beautiful names, so call upon Him by them" — Sūrah al-A'rāf 7:180. Knowing our Lord by His names is the beginning of loving Him.

A da'wah jamiyyah devoted to spreading beneficial knowledge and inviting hearts to Allah with wisdom and mercy.
