About Ikram

"Honour, generosity, and care — the meaning of Ikram — guide everything we do."

Founded in 2026, Ikram is a non-profit Islamic da'wah association based in the Maldives. We exist to make authentic Islamic knowledge accessible, to nurture sincere worship, and to support a generation that lives its faith with confidence and compassion.

From structured lessons and our academy to community outreach and youth sport, our work is rooted in mercy, moderation, and excellence — meeting people where they are and walking with them.

Learn With Us

A place to grow in knowledge

Ikram Academy offers a guided curriculum for learners at every level — from the fundamentals of reading the Qur'an to advanced studies in the Islamic sciences.

  • Qur'an recitation & memorisation (Hifz)
  • Tajweed, Tafseer & Arabic language
  • Fiqh, Aqeedah & Seerah foundations
  • Courses for children, youth & adults
  • In-person circles and online learning
